## We cannot resend emails

If you cannot find the original VPS deployment email, EDIS Global cannot resend that message or recover the initial password.

<Info>
  EDIS Global generates VPS passwords during deployment and does not retain them in a recoverable form in its management or support systems. You can set a new password using the recovery guides below, or reinstall a new VPS if it contains no data you need to keep.
</Info>

## Before you reinstall

1. Check your **spam**, **junk**, and **quarantine** folders for an email from [support@edisglobal.com](mailto:support@edisglobal.com).
2. Make sure you are checking the email address registered to your EDIS Global account.
3. Log in to the VPS Management Portal and confirm that the VPS appears under **Services** and its status is **Active**.

<Note>
  If the VPS is missing from your account or is still being provisioned, do not reinstall it. [Contact our support team](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact) so we can check the service status.
</Note>

## Choose a recovery method

If the VPS already contains data or configuration you want to keep, start with the password-reset guide for its operating system:

- [Reset a Linux root password](/vps-management/reset-root-password-linux)
- [Reset a Windows Administrator password](/vps-management/reset-windows-admin-password)

These procedures set a new password; they do not retrieve the original one. Read the prerequisites and limitations in the relevant guide before proceeding.

For a newly deployed VPS with nothing to preserve, you can instead reinstall it as described below.

## Reinstall an empty VPS

<Warning>
  Reinstalling permanently deletes the operating system, configuration, and all data currently stored on the VPS. Continue only if there is no data you need to preserve.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your VPS">
    In the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php), open **Services** and select the affected VPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the reinstallation">
    On **Server Information**, click **Reinstall OS** in **Server management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an operating system and password">
    Select Linux or Windows and enter a strong new password when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the installation">
    Start the installation and wait for the VPS to finish provisioning before attempting to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with your new password">
    Use [SSH for Linux](/getting-started/linux-secure-shell-ssh) or [Remote Desktop for Windows](/getting-started/windows-remote-desktop).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CtaButton label="View the Reinstallation Guide" link="/vps-management/autoinstall-reinstall-install-vps-linux-windows" />

<Info>
  EDIS Global does not send a new credentials email after a reinstallation. Use the password you selected during setup and store it securely in a password manager.
</Info>

## Why the original password cannot be recovered

EDIS Global generates the initial password during VPS deployment and configures it directly on the server. EDIS Global does not retain a recoverable copy in its management or support systems, so our team cannot retrieve or resend it later.

This reduces the risk of a VPS password being disclosed through those systems. If the initial password is unavailable, use the appropriate password-reset guide above or reinstall a VPS whose contents you no longer need.

## Still need help?

Our support team can help with account access, service status, and the reinstallation process. For security reasons, the team cannot recover the original VPS password or log in to the server on your behalf.

[Learn how our support team can help](/getting-started/how-our-support-team-helps-you), or [contact EDIS Global](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact).
