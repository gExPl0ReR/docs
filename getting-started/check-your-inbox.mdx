## Your VPS deployment email

After payment is confirmed and deployment is complete, EDIS Global sends the VPS access details to the email address registered with your client account.

Look for a message from [**support@edisglobal.com**](mailto:support@edisglobal.com). Keep this email secure because it contains sensitive access information.

<CtaButton label="Open VPS Management Portal" link="https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php" />

## What the email contains

| Information | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Login credentials** | The username and initial password required to access your VPS. |
| **Network details** | Your primary IPv4 address and assigned IPv6 range. |
| **VPS Management Portal** | Access to power controls, reinstallation, VNC, traffic information, upgrades, and other management functions. |
| **Important links** | Documentation, the Acceptable Use Policy, service status, and support information. |

<Warning>
  EDIS Global does not store or recover VPS passwords. The initial credentials cannot be resent if the deployment email is lost.
</Warning>

## Complete your initial setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the VPS Management Portal">
    Sign in to the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php), open **Services**, and confirm that your new VPS is shown as active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to your VPS">
    Use [SSH for Linux](/getting-started/linux-secure-shell-ssh) or [Remote Desktop for Windows](/getting-started/windows-remote-desktop) with the credentials from the deployment email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure your access">
    Change the initial operating-system password after your first successful login. For Linux, we recommend configuring SSH key authentication and following the [SSH Security Hardening Guide](/getting-started/ssh-security-hardening-guide).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to service updates">
    Subscribe to updates for the locations you use so that you receive relevant maintenance and incident notifications. See [Subscribe to Status Updates](/getting-started/statuspage-subscribe).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan your backups">
    VPS customers are responsible for protecting their own data. Review the [Backup Guide](/getting-started/backups) before placing important workloads on the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Included Traffic Pool starter credit

Every newly purchased VPS adds **100 GB of complimentary traffic** to your shared Traffic Pool. The credit does not expire while it remains in the Pool and can be assigned manually or used with Auto-Hydration.

## No deployment email received?

First check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you are checking the email address registered with your EDIS Global account.

If the VPS is active but the deployment email cannot be found, follow the [No email received?](/getting-started/no-email-received) guide. Because EDIS Global does not store VPS passwords, you will need to reinstall the VPS and choose a new password.

<Warning>
  Reinstalling a VPS permanently deletes its current contents. Confirm that no required data exists on the server before starting a reinstallation.
</Warning>
