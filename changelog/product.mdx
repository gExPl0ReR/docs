## Product updates

<Update label="August 2026">
  ### August 18, 2026 — Credit card charge timing updated 💳📅

  We've adjusted **when automatic credit card charges** happen for renewal invoices.

  **What's changed**

  - Previously: your card was charged **1 day after invoice creation**.

  👉 Learn more in the [VPS Billing Workflow](/faq/billing-lifecycle/automated-vps-billing-workflow) and [How VPS Renewals Work](/faq/billing-lifecycle/how-vps-renewals-work).

<Update label="May 2026">
  ### May 5, 2026 — UEFI, BIOS, and vTPM settings for VPS ⚙️🔐

  EDIS Global VPS now exposes **Boot Mode** and **TPM** controls directly in the System Configuration, making it easier to deploy modern operating systems like **Windows 11** and **Windows Server 2025**.

  **What’s new**

  - **Boot Mode**: choose between **BIOS** (default, legacy) or **UEFI** (modern, required for Secure Boot and newer Windows releases).
  - **TPM**: leave **Disabled** (default) or enable **Software TPM** for OS features that require a Trusted Platform Module — such as Windows 11 installation and disk encryption.

  **Defaults & automation**

  - Defaults remain **BIOS \+ TPM Disabled** for maximum compatibility across Linux workloads.
  - **Auto-Installers** continue to pick the correct Boot Mode and TPM setting automatically — manual values are ignored to guarantee a successful install.
  - Manual settings apply when deploying **custom Cloud-Init images** or **uploaded ISOs**.

  👉 Full details in the [UEFI vs BIOS and vTPM guide](/vps-management/uefi-vs-bios-and-v-tpm).
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026">
  ### January 12, 2026 — Smarter 90% traffic warnings for auto-hydration 🚦📊

  Previously, **90% traffic usage warnings** were sent every time a trigger threshold was reached — even when **Auto-Hydration** was enabled.

  For customers with **high traffic consumption**, this could result in repeated warning emails shortly after each automatic traffic assignment, despite having **sufficient traffic pool balance** and no risk of throttling.

  **Improvement**

  Starting **January 12, 2026**, we no longer send **90% traffic limit warning emails** when:

  - **Auto-Hydration is enabled**, and
  - the account has **enough traffic pool balance** to cover the next automatic hydration.

  This ensures traffic warnings are only sent when **action is actually required**, reducing unnecessary notifications while keeping customers fully informed when there is a real risk of traffic throttling.
</Update>

<Update label="December 2025">
  ### December 28, 2025 — Due-date reminders for partially paid invoices 💳⏰

  EDIS Global applies **account credit automatically during billrun** (unless you’ve opted out of automatic credit application).<br />If the available credit is **not sufficient**, the invoice is **partially paid** and remains **unpaid** until the outstanding amount is settled.

  Starting **December 28, 2025**, we send **payment reminders at the service due date** for invoices that:

  - received a **partial credit payment**, and
  - still have an **outstanding amount due**.

  This helps ensure important renewals don’t get missed when only a small remainder is left to pay.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2025">
  ### September 25, 2025 — Post-install scripts deprecated ⚠️➡️ Cloud-Init only

  We’ve officially **retired Post-Install Scripts** from the EDIS Global VPS platform.<br />All new operating system installations now rely exclusively on **Cloud-Init**, the industry standard for automated server initialization.

  👉 Why this change?

  - **Unified automation**: Cloud-Init replaces custom Post-Install scripts with a standardized, more powerful configuration method.
  - **Faster deployments**: Inject configs directly at installation — no need for extra script execution afterwards.
  - **Broader compatibility**: Supported across nearly all Linux distributions we offer.
  - **Future-ready**: Easier scaling, improved security, and cleaner workflows.

  If you've used Post-Install Scripts before, don't worry — we've kept the [archived documentation](/vps-management/post-install-scripts) for reference.<br />For all current and future VPS setups, please use our [Cloud-Init Installation Guide](/vps-management/edis-cloud-init-vps-installation).
</Update>
