## EDIS Global VPS reselling & discount structure

Run customer services on standard EDIS Global VPS plans, manage them under your account and automate ordering and operations through the APIs. Volume discounts become available as your active VPS count grows.

---

### Volume-based discounts

Our reseller discounts are based on the number of active VPS services in your account. The more you manage, the higher your discount level.

| **Tier** | **Requirement** | **Discount** |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Tier 1** | 50 active VPS services | **5 %** |
| **Tier 2** | 100 active VPS services | **10 %** |
| **Tier 3** | 150+ active VPS services | **20 %** |

Once we confirm your reseller tier, your discount automatically applies to all future VPS orders placed using your reseller coupon code.

Below 50 active VPS, standard pricing applies. Coupon eligibility does not automatically reprice existing services or apply discounts retrospectively.

### API access for enhanced management

The Order API is available after registration, subject to normal account verification. Management API access requires at least one VPS on the account. Use the APIs to integrate ordering and operations into your own customer platform; keep your EDIS Global credentials in your server-side systems.

### Becoming a reseller (application & eligibility)

Becoming an EDIS Global VPS reseller is simple.  
You can start right away by ordering services directly on our website. Once your account reaches **50 active VPS services**, contact our team. We’ll provide you with a **unique coupon code** that unlocks the **first reseller discount tier**.  

No formal application is required; your activity and growth within the platform are what qualify you for reseller status.

---

### Division of support roles

As a reseller, you act as the primary point of contact for your end customers, handling all customer-facing communication, billing, and general inquiries.  

EDIS Global provides **infrastructure-level technical support** directly to you, the reseller. Our team assists with backend issues, feature requests, and **payment-related matters concerning your reseller account**, ensuring you can deliver a smooth and reliable service to your clients.

---

### Loyalty perks & discounts

As a gesture of appreciation, we occasionally offer loyalty perks such as complimentary access to our bespoke IP address service, early access to new VPS locations, and additional reseller-only benefits.

### Reseller responsibilities

Maintain a monitored abuse contact, investigate valid complaints and act within **24 hours**. You remain responsible for payment, administration, security, subscriber support and abuse originating from your customers, including where subscribers have no access to the EDIS Global portal.

The 24-hour requirement is **not a guaranteed grace period**. Serious or ongoing attacks, phishing, malware, scanning, spam or immediate network risk may require immediate filtering, port blocking, suspension or other restrictions. Reply with the action taken and measures to prevent recurrence. See [Reporting abuse](/misc/reporting-abuse) and the [Acceptable Use Policy](/getting-started/acceptable-use-policy).

### Payments and renewals

Prepaid [account credit](/faq/billing-lifecycle/how-to-add-account-credit) can simplify automated orders and renewals. Credit is applied to newly generated renewal invoices by default; invoices that already exist require manual credit application.

The Order API's `applycredit: false` controls the individual order only. If you need to review renewal invoices before paying, contact support about the [account-level credit arrangement](/faq/billing-lifecycle/pay-invoices-from-credit-balance#can-i-disable-automatic-credit-application-to-renewal-invoices). We recommend automatic renewal and [cancelling VPS services you no longer need](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service).

### Planning large deployments

Contact us with locations, VPS quantities, resource requirements, expected IN + OUT traffic, IP-prefix requirements and rollout timing so capacity can be agreed in advance. Connectivity is shared; dedicated Internet access and custom committed-bandwidth arrangements are not available for VPS deployments.
