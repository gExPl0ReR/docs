## Mount an ISO image on your VPS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your VPS Management Portal">
    Open the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate your VPS in the Services tab" />

  <Step title='Click "Mount ISO"' />
</Steps>

To select and attach an ISO file, either pick an ISO from the EDIS Global ISO library or upload a personalized ISO with the Custom ISO feature. Once mounted, select the boot device inside a noVNC session and proceed with the installation.
