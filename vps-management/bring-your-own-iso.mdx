## Upload and install your own ISO

Boot a bare-metal backup of any server on your KVM. You can also provide custom distributions. You can run any i386 or AMD64 compatible operating system on your KVM.

Use your own custom ISO file to manually install your VPS from the KVM management panel.

![Upload Custom ISO](/assets/vps-upload-iso.webp)

## Upload your ISO

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your VPS Management Portal">
    Open the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate your VPS in the Services tab" />

  <Step title='Click "Mount ISO"' />

  <Step title="Enter a valid, non-redirecting download URL">
    Enter a direct HTTP or HTTPS link to your ISO file. For example: `https://example.com/image.iso`

    A non-redirecting link points straight to the ISO file and does not pass through any other pages or redirects. A direct link gives you a faster, more reliable download and easier verification.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the download">
    Click **Download ISO** and wait until the download finishes completely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the download completes, your ISO is available to mount from the **Mount ISO** dialog. The uploaded file is renamed and appears as `xxxx/iso/KVM_xxxxx_xxxxxx.iso`.

<Note>
  A custom ISO file is available for 24 hours and is then removed from your account.
</Note>
