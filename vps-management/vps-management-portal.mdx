## Complete control of your VPS

The EDIS Global VPS Management Portal gives you direct control over your virtual servers from one convenient dashboard. Restart a server, deploy a new operating system, open a console, monitor resource usage, manage traffic and automate routine operations without waiting for manual assistance.

![EDIS Global VPS Management Portal with server controls, traffic management and monitoring](/images/edis-global-management-portal-demo.webp "EDIS Global VPS Management Portal")

- [Power Cycle](/vps-management/power-cycle-or-reboot-a-vps) Start / Stop / Restart your service
- [1-Click Installation](/vps-management/autoinstall-reinstall-install-vps-linux-windows) of common Linux distributions CentOS 9, CentOS 8, CentOS 7, Debian 13 (trixie), Debian 12 (Bookworm), Debian 11 (Bullseye), Ubuntu 24.04, Ubuntu 22.04, Ubuntu 18.04, Ubuntu 16.04
- [1-Click Installation](/vps-management/autoinstall-reinstall-install-vps-linux-windows) of Windows Server 2025, 2022, 2019 (180-days trial), and Windows 10 / 11 (90-days trial)
- [Upload your own ISO](/vps-management/bring-your-own-iso) (we are cloud-init ready), or mount provided OS ISOs
- [Reverse DNS](/faq/ip-addresses/setting-up-reverse-pointers) PTR record delegation for IPv4 and IPv6
- [VNC Server](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server) Access your VPS with a desktop VNC client
- [Console](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server#novnc-console-instructions) Access your VPS through the provided web-based noVNC Console
- Screenshot: Pull a live screenshot from your server (without launching VNC)
- [System Configuration](/vps-management/system-configuration-dialog) Customize important hardware emulation settings (ethernet, display, boot timeout, cores vs sockets, vlan)
- [Post-Install Scripts](/vps-management/post-install-scripts) (automate your deployment)
- CPU statistics, Traffic statistics
- [Detailed Traffic Information](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-stats-refill-explained) Plan traffic, used traffic, traffic refill/reset days
- [Traffic Pool](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool) Traffic Zone, Traffic Pool Size, Manual Hydration Buttons, Toggle Auto Hydration, Hydration Log per VPS and Traffic Pool balance sheet for your entire account.
- [Early Renewal & Billing Cycle Change](/faq/billing-lifecycle/how-vps-renewals-work#renew-instantly-from-the-portal) Renew a service before its due date with the **Create Invoice / Renew** button and select the billing cycle you want to renew for
- [Order API](/order-api-reference/order-api-welcome) Automate the entire product lifecycle, including configuration, ordering and cancellations.
- [Management API](/management-api-reference/authentication/create-auth-session) Automate and remote-control every technical aspect of your VPS hosting plans.
- Your server's [Support PIN](/vps-management/your-support-pin)

_Portal shown with fictional demo data._

## Monitor performance and manage traffic

Understand how your VPS is performing and stay ahead of traffic limits directly from the portal.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Usage statistics" icon="chart-line" href="/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-stats-refill-explained">
    Review CPU and traffic charts, included plan traffic, current usage and the next refill date.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Global Traffic Pool" icon="globe" href="/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool">
    View your pool balance, assign traffic manually, configure Auto-Hydration and inspect the hydration history for each VPS.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Traffic stored in your Traffic Pool does not expire. You can assign it to any VPS in your account. With **Auto-Hydration**, the portal assigns additional traffic automatically when a VPS reaches 99% of its available allowance, provided sufficient Pool Traffic is available.
