## Does EDIS Global offer free VPS trials?

No. EDIS Global does not offer free VPS hosting trials.

If you want to evaluate our service, you can purchase a regular VPS plan and test it during the first 24 hours. An eligible order may qualify for a refund if you cancel the VPS with immediate effect and submit a complete refund request within 24 hours of purchase.

<Note>
  The 24-hour refund policy is not an unconditional free trial. Eligibility restrictions apply, and only one refund is available per customer within any 12-month period.
</Note>

## How to test before buying

You can test performance and connectivity before ordering with our public tools:

1. **Ping Gateways** – [View the available gateway test IPs](http://manage.edis.at/whmcs/stgfefew_net_list.php).\
   Not every IP range is listed. Contact our team if you need to test a specific location.
2. **Latency Test** – [Measure latency to EDIS Global locations](https://latency.edis.global) and identify the most responsive location for your connection.
3. **Looking Glass Servers** – [Run network tests from our worldwide locations](https://www.edisglobal.com/blog/looking-glass#list-of-our-worldwide-looking-glass-servers).
4. **Location Map** – [Explore EDIS Global VPS locations on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1OMZyb6w3t_MNEQ-XYb8QlFjwqio&usp=sharing).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is there no free VPS trial?

Infrastructure usage and payment-provider costs make free trials impractical. Instead, eligible regular VPS orders are covered by our 24-hour refund policy.

### Can I test latency before purchasing?

Yes. Use our [Latency Test](https://latency.edis.global) or one of our [Looking Glass servers](https://www.edisglobal.com/blog/looking-glass#list-of-our-worldwide-looking-glass-servers).

### What if the VPS does not meet my needs?

Review the eligibility conditions, cancel the VPS with immediate effect, and submit the required information within 24 hours of purchase. EDIS Global reviews each request under the refund policy.
