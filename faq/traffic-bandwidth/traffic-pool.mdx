## One traffic pool. One global price.

The EDIS Global Traffic Pool gives every VPS in your account access to one shared traffic reserve. Purchase traffic once and assign it to any VPS whenever you need additional traffic.

Traffic that remains in your Pool **does not expire**. In the portal, use the **+100 GB**, **+500 GB**, **+1 TB**, or **+10 TB** quick buttons to assign traffic manually. Alternatively, enable **Auto-Hydration** for an individual VPS and select a **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB**, or **500 GB** increment. Each time the VPS reaches **99%** of its current traffic allowance, the selected increment is assigned automatically from the shared Traffic Pool.

🌍 **No traffic zones. One global price across all 50 EDIS locations.**

<CtaButton label="Buy Traffic" link="https://www.edisglobal.com/buy-traffic" />

<Warning>
  Traffic never expires while it remains in your Traffic Pool. Once Pool Traffic is assigned to a VPS, it becomes part of the current allowance for that VPS. Any assigned traffic left unused at the next monthly refill expires together with the monthly Plan Traffic.
</Warning>

## Plan Traffic and Pool Traffic explained

| Traffic type | Where it is available | What happens at the VPS monthly refill? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan Traffic** | Included with one VPS for its current monthly cycle | Usage resets and the plan allowance is refilled. Unused Plan Traffic does not carry forward. |
| **Unassigned Pool Traffic** | Shared across all VPS services in your account | It remains in your Pool and never expires. |
| **Assigned Pool Traffic** | Added to the current allowance of one VPS | Any unused assigned amount expires when that VPS reaches its next monthly refill. |
| **100 GB starter credit** | Added to your shared Pool when a new VPS is purchased | It never expires while it remains unassigned in your Pool. |

<Note>
  Only assign the amount a VPS is likely to use before its next monthly refill. Keeping the remainder in your Pool preserves it for future use.
</Note>

## Traffic Pool starter credit

Every newly purchased VPS adds **100 GB of complimentary traffic** to your Traffic Pool account. The starter credit provides a safety reserve and lets you try manual assignment or Auto-Hydration before purchasing additional Pool Traffic.

Your 100 GB starter credit:

- ✅ Never expires while stored in your Traffic Pool.
- ✅ Can be assigned to any VPS in your account.
- ✅ Works with manual assignment and Auto-Hydration.
- ✅ Can be combined with purchased Pool Traffic.

## How to use the EDIS Traffic Pool

### 1. [Purchase traffic](https://edisglobal.com/buy-traffic)

Choose the amount you need, from **1 TB** to **10 PB**. Larger bundles provide a lower price per TB. Purchased traffic is added to your shared Pool and does not expire while it remains there.

### 2. [Assign traffic manually](#traffic-pool-in-the-traffic-information-window)

Open the **Traffic Information** window for the VPS and select **+100 GB**, **+500 GB**, **+1 TB**, or **+10 TB**. The assigned amount is deducted immediately from your Pool balance and added to the current traffic allowance for that VPS.

<Warning>
  Assigned Pool Traffic does not carry over beyond the next monthly refill for that VPS. Check the refill date before assigning a large amount.
</Warning>

### 3. [Enable Auto-Hydration](#traffic-pool-in-the-traffic-information-window)

Enable **Auto-Hydration** for an individual VPS and select an increment of **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB**, or **500 GB**. When the VPS reaches **99%** of its current allowance, the selected amount is assigned automatically from your Traffic Pool. The current allowance includes monthly Plan Traffic plus any Pool Traffic already assigned during the cycle.

<Note>
  Auto-Hydration uses your existing Traffic Pool balance. It does not automatically purchase additional traffic. If the Pool balance is insufficient, no further traffic can be assigned until the Pool is topped up.
</Note>

## Traffic Pool in the Traffic Information window

The **Traffic Information** window combines current-cycle traffic data for this VPS with the shared Traffic Pool balance and assignment controls for your account. Use it to see how close the VPS is to its allowance, when Plan Traffic will refill, and whether Pool Traffic is available to assign.

![EDIS Global Traffic Information dashboard with usage charts and Traffic Pool controls](/images/edis-global-traffic-pool-controls.webp "EDIS Global Traffic Information and Traffic Pool controls")

_Portal shown with fictional demo data._

### Traffic data shown for this VPS

- **Cumulative Traffic:** Shows traffic consumed during the current cycle against the total current allowance for the VPS.
- **Daily Traffic:** Shows traffic consumption by day so you can identify usage patterns and spikes.
- **Traffic included in plan:** The Plan Traffic supplied with the VPS for each monthly cycle.
- **Hydration level:** Additional Pool Traffic currently assigned to this VPS for the current cycle.
- **Total traffic available:** The current allowance: Plan Traffic plus assigned Pool Traffic.
- **Total traffic used:** Traffic consumed during the current cycle, shown in GB and as a percentage of the current allowance.
- **Traffic reset/refill:** The number of days until Plan Traffic refills and the current-cycle allowance resets.
- **Traffic Zone:** The pricing zone that applies to Traffic Pool assignments. EDIS Global uses one Global Traffic Zone across all locations.

### Traffic Pool controls

- **Traffic Pool:** Shows the unassigned traffic currently available across your account.
- **Top up:** Opens the [Traffic Pool purchase page](https://edisglobal.com/buy-traffic).
- **Hydration log:** Shows manual and automatic traffic assignments.
- **Manual Hydration:** Provides **+100 GB**, **+500 GB**, **+1 TB**, and **+10 TB** quick-assignment buttons.
- **Auto-Hydration:** Provides **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB**, and **500 GB** automatic increments, configurable separately for every VPS.

Both manual and automatic assignments use the existing Pool balance. Auto-Hydration does not purchase additional traffic and runs only when the Pool contains enough traffic for the selected increment.

For custom monitoring, use the Management REST API callback feature to trigger notifications at a defined threshold.

## Traffic Pool accounting balance sheet

The balance sheet records every Traffic Pool transaction chronologically, including purchases, complimentary starter credits, manual or automatic assignments to VPS services, and the resulting Pool balance.

### How to read the balance sheet

- **Date/Time:** When the transaction occurred.
- **Source:** What caused the transaction, such as a purchase, starter credit, or assignment to a VPS.
- **Change:** The amount added to (`+`) or deducted from (`-`) the Pool.
- **New Balance:** The Pool balance after the transaction.

The newest transactions appear first. A deduction records traffic being **assigned to a VPS**; it does not mean the VPS has already consumed that traffic.

## Email notification when 20% or less remains

After each Traffic Pool top-up, the resulting Pool balance becomes the reference balance for the low-balance notification. When **80% or more of that reference balance has been assigned**, EDIS Global sends an email indicating that **20% or less remains**.

Because traffic is assigned in fixed increments, the balance may already be below exactly 20%—or even at **0 GB**—when the notification is generated. The email indicates that the low-balance threshold has been crossed.

<Note>
  A low or empty Pool balance does not affect a VPS that still has available assigned traffic. It means that manual assignment and Auto-Hydration cannot provide further traffic until the Pool is topped up.
</Note>

## Which option should you choose?

| Situation | Recommended option |
| --- | --- |
| Temporary or unpredictable traffic increase | Assign or purchase Pool Traffic. |
| Existing Pool balance is sufficient | Assign traffic manually or enable Auto-Hydration. |
| Traffic regularly runs out before the monthly refill | Compare and upgrade to a higher VPS plan. |
| The next monthly refill is very close | Assign only what you expect to use before the refill, or wait for the Plan Traffic reset. |
| You already use the largest available VPS plan | Use the Traffic Pool for additional traffic. |
