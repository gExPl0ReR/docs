## All about VPS traffic, metering and refills

This page explains how to access and interpret traffic information for your VPS in the VPS Management Portal. It covers total traffic used, available (remaining) traffic, and monthly reset/refills. It also explains the cumulative traffic chart and where to find the next refill. You can also view daily traffic data for every VPS.

### Access your VPS traffic information

To access your VPS traffic information:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the portal">
    [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a VPS">
    Open a service under **Services → My Services**. On **Server Information**, scroll down to the **Traffic Information** panel beneath **Information**. On a narrow screen, the panels may be stacked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In this example, **4,096 GB plan traffic + 500 GB assigned pool traffic = 4,596 GB current allowance**. With **1,100 GB used**, **3,496 GB remains** until the next refill. The **1,500 GB shared pool balance** is separate: it has not yet been assigned to this VPS.


### Total traffic used

The total amount of traffic (GB and percentage) consumed since the beginning of the current **monthly service cycle**.

### Total traffic available

The current total traffic allowance for the VPS during the monthly cycle. It consists of:

- the monthly Plan Traffic included with the VPS; and
- any Pool Traffic assigned manually or through Auto-Hydration during the current cycle.

Assigning Pool Traffic increases this value immediately.

### Monthly traffic reset/refill

The number of days until the VPS begins a new monthly traffic cycle. At the refill:

- the usage counter resets;
- the monthly Plan Traffic allowance is replenished; and
- any unused Pool Traffic already assigned to the VPS expires.

Traffic that remains unassigned in the shared Traffic Pool does not expire.

### How EDIS Global meters traffic

EDIS Global measures traffic usage directly on the Ethernet interfaces of your Virtual Private Servers in real time.

EDIS Global calculates total traffic as the sum of **ingress (incoming)** and **egress (outgoing)** traffic across the measured period.

$Total Traffic  =  \sum (\text{IN} + \text{OUT})$

### What happens as traffic is consumed?

EDIS Global sends an informational notice at **80%** and a warning at **90%** of the current allowance. EDIS Global suppresses the 90% warning when Auto-Hydration is enabled and the Traffic Pool contains enough traffic for the next automatic assignment.

At **99%**, Auto-Hydration assigns the selected **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB**, or **500 GB** increment, provided the Pool balance is sufficient. The current total allowance then increases by the assigned amount.

EDIS Global provides an approximately **5% grace allowance** before rate-limiting. If no additional traffic becomes available, the VPS Ethernet interface is limited to approximately **1 Mbps** at approximately **105% usage**. EDIS Global sends an action-required notification when it applies the rate limit.

There is no automatic overage charge, and the VPS remains online. Review all [restoration options](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/exceeding-monthly-traffic-limit-edis-global) if the allowance has been exhausted.


### Cumulative and daily traffic charts

On **Server Information**, scroll below the management panels to find the charts:

- **Cumulative Traffic** shows the total used during the displayed cycle. Its vertical scale is logarithmic: equal spacing can represent 1, 10, 100 or 1,000 GB, rather than equal increases in usage.
- **Daily Traffic** shows how much traffic was used on each day, helping you spot busy days.
- The red allowance line in the cumulative chart shows the current traffic allowance. It moves higher when Pool Traffic is assigned.

For the next refill date or countdown, check the **Traffic Information** panel. Use the remaining allowance and time until refill to decide whether to [assign Pool Traffic](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool), upgrade the VPS plan, or wait for the next refill. Hover over the live charts in the portal for detailed readings.
