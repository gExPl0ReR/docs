## 🚀 How to upgrade your disk space

Drive Size Boost adds storage **on top of the disk space included with your VPS plan**. It is available for every VPS plan at checkout and can also be added later to an existing VPS.

You can add up to **400 GB per VPS**. For example, a KVM Premium plan with 100 GB plus a 400 GB Drive Size Boost provides a **500 GB virtual drive**.

## ✅ Add Drive Size Boost to an existing VPS

1. Log in to the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php).
2. Open the VPS under **Services**.
3. Go to **Configurable Options** and select **Upgrade Resources**.
4. Under **Drive Size Boost**, select the required amount from 10 GB to 400 GB.
5. Confirm the change and pay the prorated invoice.

If the self-service option is unavailable for a particular service, contact support and include the VPS IP address or KVM ID.

## ❗️We never touch your partition

EDIS Global enlarges the virtual drive but does **not** resize your partitions or filesystem. Complete a cold boot after activation, then extend the existing partition and filesystem or create a separate volume inside the operating system.

<Note>
  Back up your data before changing partitions or filesystems. Follow the [Linux disk-resize guide](/advanced-setup-guides/manually-resize-disk-after-plan-upgrade-on-live-linux-systems) or [Windows disk-extension guide](/advanced-setup-guides/extend-hard-drive-windows-guide).
</Note>
