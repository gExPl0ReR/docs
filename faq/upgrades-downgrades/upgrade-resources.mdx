## 🧩 Upgrade VPS resources (RAM, CPU, disk, IP addresses)

Need more resources without changing your entire plan?\
You can add more **RAM, vCPU cores, disk space (Drive Boost)** or **IP addresses** to your VPS.

---

## 🆚 What's the difference?

This is **not** a plan change. It's a **resource extension** of your current VPS.

You can:

- Add up to **24 GB RAM**
- Add up to **8 vCPU cores**
- Add up to **400 GB disk space** with Drive Size Boost
- Add up to **10 additional IPv4 addresses**
- Mix and match any of the above

The configurable options currently available are:

| Resource | Available additions | Monthly price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| RAM | +1 to +10 GB, +16 GB or +24 GB | EUR 5.00 per GB |
| vCPU | Up to +8 vCPU cores | EUR 10.00 per core |
| Drive Size Boost | +10, +20, +30, +40, +50, +100, +200, +300 or +400 GB | EUR 3.60 to EUR 116.00 |
| IPv4 | 1 to 10 additional addresses | EUR 5.00 per address |

Drive Size Boost is added to the plan's included storage. A plan with 100 GB plus the maximum 400 GB boost therefore provides a 500 GB virtual drive. See the [complete Drive Size Boost pricing and activation guide](./upgrade-disk-drive-boost).

---

## 💶 How is it billed?

Just like plan upgrades:\
You only pay the **prorated amount** for the rest of your billing cycle.

---

## 🛒 How to order a resource upgrade

### Option a – order via support

1. Contact our support team
2. Share your VPS IP or KVM\_ID
3. We'll send you a personalized upgrade link immediately

### Option b – order via portal

1. Log in to your [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php)
2. Find your VPS in the **Services** tab
3. Go to **Configurable Options**
4. Click **“Upgrade Resources”** (your personalized link)
5. Select what you want to upgrade
6. Confirm and pay the invoice

---

## 🛠️ What happens after payment?

- We process the upgrade within **7 minutes** after payment confirmation
- You’ll receive an **email with activation steps**
- **RAM and vCPU** require a **cold reboot**
- **IP addresses** need to be manually configured inside your OS
- **Drive Size Boost** enlarges the virtual drive. EDIS Global does not resize your partitions or filesystem; after the required cold boot, you decide when to extend the existing filesystem or create a separate volume.

## Need more traffic than our plans offer?

If you already use the largest tier, add traffic from your shared Traffic Pool. In the portal, assign **+100 GB**, **+500 GB**, **+1 TB**, or **+10 TB** manually, or configure Auto-Hydration to assign **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB**, or **500 GB** automatically at 99% usage. Auto-Hydration requires a sufficient Pool balance and does not purchase traffic automatically. **One global Traffic Pool price applies across all 50 EDIS locations.**
