## Configure PTR records (rDNS)&#x20;

You can configure reverse DNS or PTR records for both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses in the VPS Management Portal.

![Setting up Reverse Pointers](/assets/vps-reverse-dns.webp)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your">
    [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate your VPS in the Services tab" />

  <Step title="Click Reverse DNS to administer PTR records ">
    "**Set**" configures and "**Unset**" removes a record from our DNS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automate PTR management

Use the Management API to [retrieve](/management-api-reference/ptr/get-ptr-records), [set](/management-api-reference/ptr/set-ptr-records) or [delete](/management-api-reference/ptr/delete-ptr-records) PTR records.

## External reverse-DNS delegation

EDIS Global hosts reverse DNS on the rCodezero Anycast DNS infrastructure. PTR management remains on that infrastructure; individual IP addresses and parts of networks are not subdelegated to external authoritative nameservers. Setting a PTR record is different from delegating a reverse-DNS zone.

## Does a VPN endpoint need a custom PTR?

A custom PTR is normally unnecessary for VPN connectivity. If your application requires a particular reverse hostname, configure its PTR through the portal or API. Contact support with the technical requirement if you need help.
