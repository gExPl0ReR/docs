EDIS Global VPS addresses are **static, dedicated datacentre IP addresses**. They remain assigned to your VPS through upgrades and reinstalls and are not shared with other customers while assigned to your service.

## Are VPS IP addresses static or dynamic?

<Note>
  Both the **IPv4 address and the /64-equivalent IPv6 allocation** assigned to
  your VPS **are "static".**
</Note>

This means that they will not change for as long as you have the virtual server.

The IP addresses also remain unchanged in case you need to reinstall your KVM virtual server, no matter how long the reinstall takes.

**Bottom line:** An IP address assigned to your VPS remains yours until you cancel your server.

<Note>
  The IPv6 allocation is not routed as a separate `/64`. Configure IPv6
  addresses with the required `/48` prefix length and use the location-specific
  gateway shown in the VPS Management Portal. See [IPv6
  Setup](/vps-management/ipv6-configuration).
</Note>

## Are static VPS IP addresses residential?

**Static describes whether an address stays the same; it does not mean residential.** Our VPS infrastructure is hosted in data centres, so we provide datacentre IP addresses, not residential or "native residential" IP addresses.

Third-party websites and apps make their own classification, reputation and access decisions. EDIS Global cannot control or override those decisions. Changing an IP address, prefix or VPS location cannot guarantee acceptance by a particular platform or prevent its account restrictions.

See [Residential IP addresses and VPS hosting](/faq/ip-addresses/residential-ip-addresses) for a detailed explanation of residential, mobile, static, dedicated and datacentre addresses.
