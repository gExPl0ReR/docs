## Add IPv4 addresses through the portal

You can order up to **10 additional IPv4 addresses** yourself in the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php). We assign available addresses randomly.

**Ordering route: Configurable Options → Upgrade Resources → Additional IPs → invoice → payment → delivery.**

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Random address assignment | Included |
| Additional IPv4 address | **€5 per month, per address** |

💎 **Bespoke IPv4** — Choose your preferred address from the available list and [**order it through our support team**](/faq/ip-addresses/order-bespoke-ip-address).

## Ordering an additional IPv4 address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your VPS">
    Log in to the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php), open **Services → My Services**, and select the VPS that needs additional addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Configurable Options">
    Select the **Configurable Options** tab, then click **Upgrade Resources**.

    <Frame caption="The Configurable Options tab and Upgrade Resources button. Portal screenshot cropped and enhanced for readability.">
      <img src="/images/portal-additional-ip-upgrade-resources.png" alt="Configurable Options tab and green Upgrade Resources button highlighted in the VPS portal" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the number of additional IPs">
    On the **Upgrade/Downgrade** page, find the **Additional IPs** row. Under **New Configuration**, choose the required number of additional addresses. Compare it with **Current Configuration**, especially if you already have additional IPs.

    Leave other resources at **No Change** unless you also intend to upgrade them. Click **Click to Continue >>**, review the order and amount, and complete the upgrade request to create the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the invoice">
    Pay the generated invoice. After we confirm your payment, we provision the additional addresses to your VPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the delivered addresses">
    We confirm the assigned IP addresses **by email** and in the portal. Return to the VPS, open **Server Information**, and click **Network Info** for the assigned addresses and network settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the addresses in your operating system">
    Add the delivered addresses to your VPS operating system and configure the services that should use them. Keep the existing primary address in place when adding an additional address.

    For Linux, follow [Configure additional IPv4 addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/configuring-additional-ip-address-linux).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The **♻ recycle icon** beside Primary IPv4 replaces the primary address. To **add** addresses, use **Configurable Options → Upgrade Resources**.
</Info>

## Find your assigned network settings

On **Server Information**, **Assigned IPs** lists the service's addresses. **Network Info** provides the settings needed to configure them. Provisioning an address to your service does not configure it inside the operating system.
