## Adding additional IP addresses

Add up to **10 additional IPv4 addresses** to your VPS. With the standard option, the system selects the addresses randomly. For a choice from available addresses, see [Bespoke IP addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/order-bespoke-ip-address).

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Random address assignment | Included |
| Additional IPv4 address | **€5 per month, per address** |

## Ordering an additional IPv4 address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your VPS and find its identifier">
    In the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php), open **Services → My Services** and select the VPS. Under **Server Information**, find **ID** or **Primary IP** above the management buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support through the messenger">
    Open the chat bubble at the bottom right of the portal. Provide the VPS's **ID (KVM_ID)** or **Primary IP**, and say how many additional IPv4 addresses you need. The recycle icon beside Primary IPv4 replaces the primary address; it does not order an additional address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order and pay using the link provided">
    We will send you an order link. Follow its ordering, checkout, and payment steps. After payment, the system provisions the additional randomly assigned IPv4 address to your service.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find and configure the assigned address

Return to the VPS's **Server Information** tab after provisioning. **Assigned IPs** lists the service's addresses. Open **Network Info** for the assigned network settings.

The additional address must also be configured inside the operating system before it can be used. Keep the primary address in place when adding another address.
