## Add IPv4 addresses through the portal

You can order up to **10 additional IPv4 addresses** yourself in the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php). We assign available addresses randomly.

**Ordering route: Configurable Options → Upgrade Resources → Additional IPs → invoice → payment → delivery.**

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Random address assignment | Included |
| Additional IPv4 address | **€5 per month, per address** |

💎 **Bespoke IPv4**: Choose your preferred address from the available list and [**order it through our support team**](/faq/ip-addresses/order-bespoke-ip-address).

## Ordering an additional IPv4 address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your VPS">
    Log in to the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php), open **Services → My Services**, and select the VPS that needs additional addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Configurable Options">
    Select the **Configurable Options** tab, then click **Upgrade Resources**.

    <Frame caption="The Configurable Options tab and Upgrade Resources button. Portal screenshot cropped and enhanced for readability.">
      <img src="/images/portal-additional-ip-upgrade-resources.png" alt="Configurable Options tab and green Upgrade Resources button highlighted in the VPS portal" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the number of additional IPs">
    On the **Upgrade/Downgrade** page, find the **Additional IPs** row. Under **New Configuration**, select the required number of additional addresses. Compare it with **Current Configuration**, especially if you already have additional IPs.

    Leave other resources at **No Change** unless you also intend to upgrade them. Click **Click to Continue >>**, review the order and amount, and complete the upgrade request to create the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the invoice">
    Pay the generated invoice. After we confirm your payment, we provision the additional addresses to your VPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the delivered addresses">
    We confirm the assigned IP addresses **by email** and in the portal. Return to the VPS, open **Server Information**, and click **Network Info** for the assigned addresses and network settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the addresses in your operating system">
    Add the delivered addresses to your VPS operating system and configure the services that should use them. Keep the existing primary address in place when adding an additional address.

    For Linux, follow [Configure additional IPv4 addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/configuring-additional-ip-address-linux).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The **♻ recycle icon** beside Primary IPv4 replaces the primary address. To **add** addresses, use **Configurable Options → Upgrade Resources**.
</Info>

## Find your assigned network settings

### Identify the primary and additional IPv4 addresses

Open **Server Information** and compare the two fields above the management buttons:

1. **Primary IP** identifies the VPS's main IPv4 address.
2. **Assigned IPs** lists **all assigned IPv4 addresses, including the primary address**.
3. Match the address shown under **Primary IP** to its entry in **Assigned IPs**. The other IPv4 entries are your additional addresses.

**Three assigned IPv4 addresses means one primary address plus two additional addresses.** The repeated primary address is not an additional IP.

<Frame caption="Anonymized example: blue identifies the same primary address in both fields; orange identifies the two additional IPv4 addresses. Highlights and labels are added for this guide.">
  <img src="/images/portal-primary-additional-ip-list.png" alt="Primary IP 192.0.2.10 highlighted in blue, including its repeated entry under Assigned IPs. The two additional addresses 198.51.100.20 and 192.0.2.30 are highlighted in orange." />
</Frame>

Additional addresses may be in a different subnet. Identify the primary address using the **Primary IP** field, rather than guessing from the address prefix or its position in the list.

### Check the addresses and settings in Network Info

Click **Network Info** in the **Server management** panel. The **Network Information** dialog lists the addresses with their netmasks. Match the primary address you identified above; the other IPv4 addresses are additional ones. The IPv6 prefix is separate from your additional IPv4 addresses.

<Frame caption="The same example addresses in Network Info: one primary IPv4 address and two additional IPv4 addresses. Addresses are anonymized; highlights are added for this guide.">
  <img src="/images/portal-network-info-additional-ips.png" alt="Network Information dialog: 192.0.2.10 is marked Primary IP, while 198.51.100.20 and 192.0.2.30 are marked as two additional IPs. Each IPv4 row includes its netmask; the IPv6 prefix is shown separately." />
</Frame>

Use **your own addresses and network settings from Network Info**, not the example values in these screenshots. The lower **Network Configuration (Details)** section contains the gateway and DNS settings.

Provisioning an address to your service does not configure it inside the operating system. **Keep the primary address configured and add the additional addresses** to the OS and any services that need them.
