## Bespoke IP address service

**You order bespoke additional IPv4 addresses through our support team by messenger or email.** Support sends you the available addresses, you choose, and the team adds your selection to your VPS after payment.

You can add up to **10 additional IPv4 addresses** to your VPS. For randomly assigned addresses you can order yourself, use the [self-service additional IP guide](/faq/ip-addresses/order-additional-ip-address).

| Service | Price per address |
| --- | --- |
| Bespoke address selection | **€10 one-time** |
| Additional IPv4 address | **€5 per month** |

We charge the one-time selection fee in addition to the recurring IP address fee.

## Ordering a bespoke IPv4 address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request bespoke additional IPs">
    Open the **messenger** using the chat bubble at the bottom right of the portal, or email our [support team](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact).

    Include your VPS's **primary IP address or KVM_ID**, the **number of additional addresses** you need, and any prefix requirements. State that you want **bespoke additional IPv4 addresses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose from the available addresses">
    Support sends you a list of available IP addresses for your VPS location, **from multiple /24 prefixes where possible**. Reply with the address or addresses you want.

    The choices depend on current inventory. If you require addresses from distinct /24 prefixes, ask support to confirm availability before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and pay the invoice">
    After you and support agree on your selection, support creates the invoice. Review it and pay. The team waits for payment confirmation before delivering the selected addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your chosen addresses">
    After the team confirms your payment, it adds the chosen additional IP address or addresses to your VPS service and confirms delivery.

    Open the VPS in the [VPS Management Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php) and select **Server Information**. Your chosen addresses appear under **Assigned IPs** alongside the existing **Primary IP**. Match the primary address to its entry in the list; the other IPv4 entries are your additional addresses.

    See [**Identify the primary and additional IPv4 addresses**](/faq/ip-addresses/order-additional-ip-address#identify-the-primary-and-additional-ipv4-addresses) for annotated portal screenshots, then open **Network Info** for the assigned network settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your operating system and services">
    Add the delivered IP addresses inside your VPS operating system, then configure the services that should use them. Keep the existing primary address in place.

    For Linux, follow [Configure additional IPv4 addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/configuring-additional-ip-address-linux). For the assigned settings, see [VPS network information](/vps-management/ip-address-information).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to send support

You can use this message:

```text
I would like to order bespoke additional IPv4 addresses.
VPS primary IP or KVM_ID: [your VPS identifier]
Number of additional IPs: [quantity]
Prefix requirements, if any: [your requirements]
Please send me the available addresses before issuing the invoice.
```

## Availability and IP expectations

Selecting an address does not guarantee access from a particular country or to a particular platform, residential IP classification, or a particular reputation-list status. See [connectivity and IP expectations](/commercial-vpn-hosting#connectivity-and-ip-expectations).

Bespoke ordering adds addresses to your service. Replacing the existing primary address is a separate [IP change](/faq/ip-addresses/change-ip-address).
