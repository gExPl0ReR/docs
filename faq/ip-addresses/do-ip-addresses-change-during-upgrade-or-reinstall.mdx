## Will my IP address change during reinstall or upgrade?

Your existing VPS keeps its IP addresses when you upgrade its plan or reinstall its operating system.

[Downgrades are technically unavailable](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/is-it-possible-to-downgrade), including after human review. Moving to a smaller plan requires a separate new VPS and manual data migration.

<Warning>
  Cancelling and re-ordering does not preserve your IP address. A new VPS receives a different, randomly selected IP, even in the same location. If keeping your current IP is essential, keep the existing VPS.
</Warning>
