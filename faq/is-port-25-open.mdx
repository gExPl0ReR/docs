## Is Port 25 open?

Yes. EDIS Global permits SMTP traffic on TCP Port 25:

- **Inbound TCP Port 25 is unrestricted.**
- **Outbound TCP Port 25 is open.** VPS instances created on or after **February 24, 2025** have a default limit of **8 packets per second**, with an **initial burst of up to 16 packets**.

The effective date is based on the creation date of the individual VPS, not the creation date of your customer account.

<Warning>
  EDIS Global has a strict no-spam policy. If unsolicited email is sent from any
  IP address assigned to a VPS, we may block outbound TCP Port 25 for the
  entire VPS. The block covers all IP addresses assigned to that VPS.
</Warning>

For exception requirements and enforcement details, see our [Port 25 SMTP policy and rate limits](/faq/port-25-smtp-rate-limit-policy).
