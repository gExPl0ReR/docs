## The short version

- Your VPS **renews automatically** unless you cancel it in the portal.
- If you don't need a service anymore, **cancel it before the next invoice is created**.
- Auto-renewal keeps your VPS running without interruption.

<Note>
  Auto-renewal is intentional. It protects your service, your data, and your uptime.
</Note>

---

## When we send invoices

We create a **Pro-Forma invoice 7 days before the due date** of your service.

A Pro-Forma invoice is like an offer:

- If you pay it, your service renews and we issue a fully compliant tax invoice.
- If you don't pay it, we send payment reminders. If it stays unpaid, the service is suspended, then terminated, and all data is wiped.

See the full timeline: [VPS Billing Workflow](/faq/billing-lifecycle/automated-vps-billing-workflow) and [Data Retention Policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/data-retention-period#data-retention-policy-at-edis-global).

## Renew instantly from the portal

Don't want to wait? You can renew a service any time.

In your client portal, open the service and click the blue **Create Invoice / Renew** button.

<Frame>
  ![Create Invoice / Renew button in the client portal](/assets/vps-renewal-create-invoice-button.png)
</Frame>

This lets you:

- Generate the renewal invoice immediately.
- Choose the billing cycle you want to renew for.

<Tip>
  **Annual billing gives you one month free.** You pay for 11 months and get 12.
</Tip>

You can also contact our team to issue an upfront invoice for you.

## Automatic payments

If you have a **credit card on file, a PayPal subscription,** or **account credit**, renewal invoices are paid automatically.

| Payment method | When it's charged |
| --- | --- |
| Credit card on file | Charged **4 days after** the invoice is sent |
| Account credit | Applied **immediately** when the invoice is issued |
| PayPal Subscription | PayPal makes a recurring payment on your behalf. |

When account credit is applied, your service renews right away.

Learn more:

- [Automated Payments](/faq/billing-lifecycle/automated-payments)

## For resellers: opt out of automatic credit application

If you work with account credit and don't want it applied to renewal invoices automatically, you can opt out.

See [Credit Manipulation](/faq/billing-lifecycle/scheduled-billing-window#-credit-manipulation).

You can request to convert the payment into account credit. Terms and conditions apply.
