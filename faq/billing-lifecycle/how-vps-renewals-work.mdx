## Automatic renewal and cancellation

<Warning>
  ### Your VPS renews automatically

  Renewal continues **unless you cancel the service in the portal**.

  **No longer need your VPS? Cancel it before the next invoice is created.**

  We create renewal invoices **7 days before your service's due date**.

  Auto-renewal is intentional. It keeps your service running and protects your data and uptime.
</Warning>

---

## When we send invoices

We create a **Pro-Forma invoice 7 days before the due date** of your service.

A Pro-Forma invoice is like an offer:

- If you pay it, your service renews and we issue a fully compliant tax invoice.
- If you don't pay it, we send payment reminders. If it stays unpaid, we suspend the service, then terminate it and wipe all data.

See the full timeline: [VPS Billing Workflow](/faq/billing-lifecycle/automated-vps-billing-workflow) and [Data Retention Policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/data-retention-period#what-is-your-data-retention-period).

## Renew instantly from the portal

Don't want to wait? You can renew a service any time.

In your client portal, open **Services → My Services** and select the VPS. Find the blue **Create Invoice / Renew** button beneath **Payment Method**, beside the service card.

<Frame caption="Fresh portal view. Account-specific billing values are hidden.">
  <img src="/images/portal-renew-button.png" alt="Manage Product page with Create Invoice / Renew highlighted beneath Payment Method" />
</Frame>

This lets you:

- Generate the renewal invoice immediately.
- Select the billing cycle you want to renew for.

<Tip>
  **The annual 11+1 offer gives you one month free.** You pay for 11 months and get 12.

  This offer uses a coupon. Purchases made with it are **not eligible for a refund under the [24-hour refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy)**.
</Tip>

You can also contact our team to issue an upfront invoice for you.

## Automatic payments

If you have a **credit card on file, a PayPal subscription,** or **account credit**, we can pay renewal invoices automatically. We apply available account credit by default unless support has configured the opt-out described below.

| Payment method | When it's charged |
| --- | --- |
| Credit card on file | Charged **4 days after** the invoice is sent |
| Account credit | Applied **immediately by default** when the invoice is issued |
| PayPal Subscription | PayPal makes a recurring payment on your behalf (at the due date). |

When account credit is applied, your service renews right away.

Learn more:

- [Automated Payments](/faq/billing-lifecycle/automated-payments)

## For resellers: opt out of automatic credit application

If you work with account credit and prefer to review renewal invoices before paying, [contact support](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact). We can configure your account to disable automatic credit application during scheduled invoice creation. This is an account-level billing arrangement. We continue to issue renewal invoices, and you must pay them by their due dates. We manage other automatic payment arrangements separately.

See [Can I disable automatic credit application to renewal invoices?](/faq/billing-lifecycle/pay-invoices-from-credit-balance#can-i-disable-automatic-credit-application-to-renewal-invoices) and [Optional credit handling during invoice creation](/faq/billing-lifecycle/scheduled-billing-window#optional-credit-handling-during-invoice-creation).
