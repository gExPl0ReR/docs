## Automated currency conversion

All our prices and payments for EDIS Global services are in **EUR (€)**. You can use various payment methods. The conversion to EUR will be handled automatically, no matter which currency you hold.

## Credit card payments
- When paying with a **Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, or other credit cards**, your bank **automatically converts** the amount from **EUR (€) to your card's currency** (e.g., USD, GBP, etc.).  
- **Your bank or card issuer** determines the exchange rate and any applicable conversion fees.

## PayPal payments
- If you select **PayPal**, the PayPal payment dialogue will display the amount in your **account currency**, and in **EUR (€)** right below.
- When completing the payment, **PayPal will automatically charge your PayPal balance in your local currency (e.g., USD, GBP, etc.)** using its **own exchange rates**.  
- You can review the exchange in the PayPal payment dialogue, before executing the transaction.

## Cryptocurrency payments

**Crypto by EDIS Global**, our own gateway, calculates the cryptocurrency amount needed to pay your EUR invoice.

1. Select **Crypto by EDIS Global** on the invoice.
2. Select a [supported currency and blockchain](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payments#supported-currencies-and-blockchains).
3. Review the exact amount, destination address, network and checkout timer.
4. Send the displayed amount, covering sending fees separately so the full amount arrives.

The quoted exchange rate stays fixed for the **12-hour payment window**. A new payment attempt may use a new rate. If the window expires, or you change the currency or network, follow the current checkout instructions.

USDT and USDC are also converted from the EUR invoice amount. **EUR 10 does not mean 10 USDT or 10 USDC.** Always use the cryptocurrency amount calculated by checkout.

EDIS Global adds no cryptocurrency-payment surcharge. Wallet, exchange and blockchain fees may still apply.

> **Important note:** Since exchange rates vary depending on the payment provider, we recommend reviewing the final amount before confirming your payment.
