## How to locate and cancel a subscription in your PayPal account

### Introduction

This article provides a step-by-step guide to help you locate and cancel a subscription in your PayPal account. Follow these instructions to manage your recurring payments effectively.

For an explanation of who initiates payments and why a subscription may continue after a service changes, see [How PayPal subscriptions work](/faq/billing-lifecycle/paypal-subscriptions-explained).

### Step-by-step guide

### 1. Log in to PayPal

Go to the [PayPal website](https://www.paypal.com) and log in with your account credentials.

### 2. Access account settings

Click the gear icon located in the top right corner to open your account settings.

### 3. Navigate to Payments

In the settings menu, click the **Payments** tab to view your payment settings.

### 4. Manage automatic payments

Open **Subscriptions and saved businesses** or **Automatic Payments** to view your subscriptions. Some interfaces show **Manage automatic payments**. Menu names may vary by country and account interface.

### 5. Select the subscription

From the list of active subscriptions, select the one you want to cancel.

### 6. Cancel the subscription

Open the subscription details and click **Cancel** to stop future recurring payments.

<Warning>
  Cancelling a PayPal subscription does not cancel your VPS or settle unpaid invoices. To stop using the VPS, also [request service cancellation](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) in the EDIS Global portal.
</Warning>

### Conclusion

By following these steps, you can easily locate and cancel any subscriptions in your PayPal account. For further assistance, visit the [PayPal Help Center](https://www.paypal.com/us/smarthelp/home).
