## How to top up your account credit

Adding funds to your account allows you to pay future invoices automatically or manually apply credit to existing invoices.

## Steps to add funds

1. **Log in** to the [Client Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php?action=addfunds).
2. Go to the **Billing** section.
3. Click **"Add Funds"**.
4. Enter the amount you want to add.
5. Select your preferred **payment method**.
6. Click **"Add Funds"** and follow the checkout process.

## How payments work

- **Renewal invoices generated after you top up your account** have available credit applied **automatically by default**. If the credit covers the invoice in full, it is marked as paid.
- **Invoices that already existed before you topped up your account** are **not** paid automatically. To use your account credit for these invoices, open the invoice and click **Apply Account Credit**.

- **Overpayments** are automatically credited to your account balance.
- **Underpayments** (when a lesser amount is received) must be resolved manually and may require an additional payment.

If you prefer to review renewal invoices before paying, support can configure your account to disable automatic credit application during scheduled invoice creation. See [Can I disable automatic credit application to renewal invoices?](/faq/billing-lifecycle/pay-invoices-from-credit-balance#can-i-disable-automatic-credit-application-to-renewal-invoices).

## Important notes for crypto payments

- Always **cover crypto transaction (TX) fees** when sending crypto payments.
- To avoid underpayment issues with cryptocurrency, it’s better to slightly **overpay** rather than risk being short by a small amount. **Overpayments** are automatically forwarded to your account balance.
- **Underpayments will require an additional payment** to get the full amount credited.

## Cancel top-up invoices

If you accidentally create one (or even multiple) top-up pro forma invoices, EDIS Global automatically cancels these documents after approximately 36 hours. You don't need to take any action. We'll take care of them automatically.
