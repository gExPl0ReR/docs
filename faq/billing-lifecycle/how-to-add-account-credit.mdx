## How to top up your account credit

<Note>
  **Buying one VPS, or unsure whether you will keep it? Pay for the service directly at checkout. You do not need to top up first.** Direct payment does not guarantee a refund. The [refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy) still applies.
</Note>

**Only top up money you intend to spend on EDIS Global services.** Account credit is a prepaid EUR balance for future purchases and renewals. It can pay new orders, future invoices automatically, or existing invoices when you apply it manually.

## Where an approved service refund goes

| How you pay for the service | Where an approved service refund goes |
| --- | --- |
| Pay the service invoice directly with cryptocurrency | Back in the same cryptocurrency and blockchain, following the refund checks |
| Top up with cryptocurrency, then pay the service invoice with account credit | Back to account credit |
| Pay the service invoice with account credit from another funding method | Back to account credit |

**If you pay for a service with account credit, any approved refund for that service returns to your account credit balance. It does not return to a bank account, card or crypto wallet. This also applies when you originally bought the credit with cryptocurrency.** Remaining credit is for future EDIS Global purchases and renewals, not withdrawal or onward transfer.

Refund eligibility and mandatory legal rights remain unchanged. Our support team reviews duplicate payments, accidental transfers and billing errors under the [incoming and outgoing payments policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/incoming-and-outgoing-payments#genuine-payment-problems-and-legal-rights).

Read the [incoming and outgoing payments policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/incoming-and-outgoing-payments) for prohibited use, risk-based verification and refund safeguards.

## Steps to add funds

1. **Log in** to the [Client Portal](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php?action=addfunds).
2. Go to the **Billing** section.
3. Click **Add Funds**.
4. Enter the amount you want to add.
5. Select your preferred **payment method**.
6. Click **Add Funds** and follow the checkout process.

## How payments work

- **Renewal invoices generated after you top up your account** have available credit applied **automatically by default**. If the credit covers the invoice in full, it is marked as paid.
- **Invoices that already existed before you topped up your account** are **not** paid automatically. To use your account credit for these invoices, open the invoice and click **Apply Account Credit**.

For cryptocurrency top-ups, the amount credited depends on the confirmed payment received. See the section below. If another payment method leaves a top-up unpaid, contact support.

If you prefer to review renewal invoices before paying, support can configure your account to disable automatic credit application during scheduled invoice creation. See [Can I disable automatic credit application to renewal invoices?](/faq/billing-lifecycle/pay-invoices-from-credit-balance#can-i-disable-automatic-credit-application-to-renewal-invoices).

## Top up with cryptocurrency

Select **Crypto by EDIS Global**, then select the currency and network in checkout. See the [supported currencies and blockchains](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payments#supported-currencies-and-blockchains).

- You can round up or overpay. Any amount received above the required payment is automatically credited to your account balance. Make sure the required amount arrives after any sending fees.
- Use the address and instructions created for this top-up invoice. There is no permanent account-credit deposit address.
- Wait for the required [blockchain confirmations](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payments-confirmation-guide-edis-global) before relying on the credit.
- If a cryptocurrency **Add Funds** payment is smaller than requested, EDIS Global adjusts the top-up invoice to the confirmed amount received. That EUR value is added to your account credit.
- Excess value from an applied payment is added to account credit. A separate payment arriving after the top-up invoice is already paid may require [manual review](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payment-help#i-sent-too-much-or-paid-twice).

An ordinary service invoice behaves differently: a partial payment reduces its outstanding balance but leaves it unpaid until the remaining amount is covered.

## Cancel top-up invoices

If you accidentally create one (or even multiple) top-up pro forma invoices, EDIS Global cancels these documents automatically if they remain unpaid. You don't need to take any action. We'll take care of them automatically.
