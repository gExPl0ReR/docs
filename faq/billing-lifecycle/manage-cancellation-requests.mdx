Changed your mind and want to keep your VPS? You can **revoke an end-of-term cancellation request yourself** in the VPS Management Portal while the service is **active or suspended**.

<Tip>
  **Quick route:** [Portal Home](https://manage.edisglobal.com/clientarea.php) → **VPS Cancellation Requests** → **Manage Cancellations** → **Revoke Cancellation**.
</Tip>

## Find an outstanding cancellation request

An individual VPS with a cancellation request displays this notice at the top of its **Manage Product** page:

> There is an outstanding cancellation request for this product/service

This means a cancellation request is recorded for that service. An **Active** status alone does not mean that a scheduled cancellation has been removed.

<Frame caption="The warning appears at the top of the individual service page.">
  <img src="/images/cancellation-service-warning.png" alt="Manage Product page with the outstanding cancellation request warning highlighted." />
</Frame>

To manage the request, click **Home** in the portal navigation to return to the main dashboard.

## Open Manage Cancellations

On the main dashboard, find **VPS Cancellation Requests** and click **Manage Cancellations**. The banner shows how many VPS cancellations can still be revoked or recovered.

<Frame caption="On the main dashboard, click Manage Cancellations. Account details and counts are hidden.">
  <img src="/images/cancellation-dashboard-anonymized.png" alt="Portal dashboard with the Manage Cancellations button highlighted in the VPS Cancellation Requests banner." />
</Frame>

You can also open the [Manage VPS cancellations page directly](https://manage.edisglobal.com/index.php?m=vpsrecovery) after signing in.

## Revoke an end-of-term cancellation

1. Find the VPS in the list. If needed, enter its **service ID, hostname or IP** in the search field and click **Search**.
2. Check the **Service**, **IP / Hostname**, **Cancellation**, **Current Status** and **Deadline / Due Date** columns to make sure you have selected the correct VPS. A scheduled end-of-term request is shown as **End of Billing Period**.
3. Click the green **Revoke Cancellation** button in that VPS's row.
4. Confirm the prompt: **“Keep this VPS and revoke its end-of-period cancellation request?”** Only confirm if you want to keep the VPS.

<Frame caption="Click Revoke Cancellation for the correct VPS. The service ID and IP address shown here are fictional; dates are hidden.">
  <img src="/images/cancellation-revoke-anonymized.png" alt="Manage VPS cancellations table with a scheduled end-of-billing-period cancellation and the Revoke Cancellation button highlighted." />
</Frame>

Revoking withdraws the scheduled cancellation request so the VPS is no longer scheduled to terminate because of that request. Review the result in the portal, then reopen the VPS's **Manage Product** page to check that the outstanding-cancellation warning has cleared.

<Warning>
  **Revoking a cancellation does not reactivate a suspended VPS.** If the service is suspended for non-payment, pay its outstanding renewal invoice to restore service.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Revoking a cancellation request makes that VPS ineligible for a refund under the [24-hour cancellation and refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy).
</Note>

## If you cancelled with immediate effect

The same **Manage VPS cancellations** page also lets you restore an **eligible VPS within six days of termination**, before its data is permanently deleted. Open the page promptly and review the action available for that service.

This is different from withdrawing an end-of-term request while the VPS is still active or suspended. Availability depends on the service's current status and eligibility. If the VPS is not listed or no suitable action is available, [contact support](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact) promptly.

After permanent deletion, the VPS data cannot be recovered. See [Data Retention Periods](/faq/billing-lifecycle/data-retention-period) for the deletion timeline.
