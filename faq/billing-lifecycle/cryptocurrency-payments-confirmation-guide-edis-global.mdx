Cryptocurrency payments through **Monero (XMR)** or **Crypto by EDIS Global** must be confirmed on the selected blockchain before EDIS Global can credit them to your invoice. A transaction appearing in your wallet or on an explorer does not necessarily mean the payment is complete.

## Monero (XMR): 10 confirmations

EDIS Global now runs its own Monero payment integration on its own infrastructure, without a third-party payment processor for XMR payments. It is separate from the Dfns-based gateway described below.

| Blockchain | Currency | Required confirmations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monero | XMR | **10 confirmations** |

The Monero checkout can detect a payment before it is ready to be credited. During this time, the invoice may still show as unpaid. Once the payment has reached **10 confirmations** and the confirmed amount covers the invoice balance, EDIS Global marks the invoice paid automatically.

This is a total confirmation requirement, not the “additional blocks after inclusion” convention used in the Dfns table below. The time required varies with the network; 10 confirmations does not mean 10 minutes. Sending the transaction or seeing it in your wallet does not by itself complete the payment.

Use the payment status in your Monero checkout to follow progress. If you have already paid, do not send another payment just because the invoice is waiting for confirmations.

## Confirmation delays for Crypto by EDIS Global

For Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron and BNB Smart Chain, our gateway uses Dfns blockchain infrastructure to detect confirmed incoming payments. Dfns defines **confirmation delay** as the number of additional blocks after the block containing the transaction, before the transaction is indexed.

The table below covers the four blockchains accepted through **Crypto by EDIS Global**. It does not apply to Monero:

| Blockchain | Accepted currencies | Additional blocks after inclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bitcoin | BTC | 2 |
| Ethereum | ETH, USDC (ERC-20), USDT (ERC-20) | 12 |
| Tron | TRX, USDT (TRC-20) | 19 |
| BNB Smart Chain (BSC) | BNB, Binance-Peg USDC and USDT (BEP-20) | 5 |

Source: [Dfns supported networks](https://docs.dfns.co/networks), checked **22 October 2026**. These indexing delays may change.

### Why Bitcoin shows 3 confirmations

For Bitcoin, the block containing the transaction counts as its first confirmation. The **2 additional blocks** in the table therefore correspond to **3 confirmations in total**. This matches the payment behavior observed with our gateway.

The table gives blocks after inclusion, not a universal wallet confirmation counter. EDIS Global marks the invoice as paid once the confirmed, credited amount covers the outstanding balance.

## How long does confirmation take?

Confirmation time depends on the blockchain, network conditions and the sending wallet or exchange. An exchange may also delay broadcasting a withdrawal. A block count does not guarantee payment within a fixed number of minutes.

Allow time for both blockchain indexing and the invoice status to update. EDIS Global begins provisioning new services after payment confirmation.

## Check your payment

1. Open the invoice and its cryptocurrency checkout to check the payment status, credited amount and any remaining balance.
2. Find the transaction ID (TXID) in your wallet or exchange withdrawal history.
3. For Monero, use the invoice's Monero checkout to check payment progress. For the other supported networks, check the transaction on the corresponding explorer:

| Blockchain | Explorer |
| --- | --- |
| Bitcoin | [Blockchain.com](https://www.blockchain.com/explorer) |
| Ethereum | [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io) |
| Tron | [Tronscan](https://tronscan.org) |
| BNB Smart Chain | [BscScan](https://bscscan.com) |

Check the destination, currency or token, network, amount and transaction status. A completed withdrawal to the wrong address, token or network cannot automatically pay the invoice.

## Confirmed transaction, but the invoice is still unpaid?

A short payment leaves an ordinary service invoice unpaid until its remaining balance is covered. A late payment or a payment sent using superseded instructions may need manual review.

If the transaction has confirmed and the invoice has not updated, contact EDIS Global Support with your invoice number, TXID, currency and blockchain. Check the [payment help guide](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payment-help) before sending another payment.

For the complete list of accepted combinations, see [supported currencies and blockchains](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cryptocurrency-payments#supported-currencies-and-blockchains).
