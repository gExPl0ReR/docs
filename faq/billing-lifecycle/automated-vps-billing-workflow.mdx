## ⏳ Simplified VPS billing workflow

**Automated billing workflow for non-payment.** This timetable shows when renewal invoices and reminders are sent, and when an unpaid VPS is suspended, terminated, and permanently deleted. Day 0 is the renewal due date.

Customer-requested cancellation follows a different timeline. See the [VPS cancellation guide](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) for immediate and end-of-term cancellation.

| Days  | Event                 | Action                                                                                              |
| ----- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| -7d   | 🟢 Invoice Created    | Renewal invoice is generated and sent                                                               |
| -3d   | 🟢 Credit Card Charge | Automatic attempt to debit your card                                                                |
| -2d   | 🟢 Friendly Reminder  | First unpaid reminder sent                                                                          |
| 0     | 🟡 Due Date           | Payment is due                                                                                      |
| \+1d  | 🟡 Overdue Reminder 1 | First overdue reminder                                                                              |
| \+2d  | 🟡 Overdue Reminder 2 | Second overdue reminder                                                                             |
| \+3d  | 🟡 Overdue Reminder 3 | Last overdue reminder                                                                               |
| \+7d  | 🟠 Service Suspension | VPS service powers down                                                                             |
| \+14d | 🔴 Termination        | VPS agreement ends <br />(service no longer active, data still present)                             |
| \+20d | 🔴 Data Deletion      | Renewal invoice is cancelled.<br />VPS data is permanently deleted 6 days after termination.<br />Recovery no longer possible. |

## 🛠 Service recovery options

### ✅ If your invoice shows unpaid

If your VPS is suspended for non-payment, pay its outstanding renewal invoice to reactivate it automatically.

If the VPS has already been terminated, contact support promptly to request recovery or reactivation before its data is permanently deleted.

The invoice detail view shows a red **UNPAID** badge:

<img src="/assets/invoice-status-unpaid-20261006.png" alt="Red UNPAID status badge in the current invoice layout" width="208" />

### ❌ If your invoice is cancelled

In the automated **non-payment workflow**, EDIS Global cancels the renewal invoice when it permanently deletes the VPS data. This happens **six days after termination**, or **20 days after the invoice due date**, as shown in the timetable above.

**At this stage, your VPS data has been permanently deleted. Recovery is no longer possible.**

The invoice detail view shows a grey **CANCELLED** badge:

<img src="/assets/invoice-status-cancelled-20261006.png" alt="Grey CANCELLED status badge in the current invoice layout" width="222" />

<Note>
  **Exception: you requested cancellation yourself**

  For immediate or end-of-term cancellation, the invoice may be cancelled before data deletion. If the invoice covers multiple services, only the VPS's charges are removed. Your VPS data remains stored for **six days after the actual termination date**.

  You can withdraw an **immediate or end-of-term cancellation** as long as the server has not been deleted, for up to **six days after termination**. Open **VPS Cancellation Requests → Manage Cancellations** on the portal dashboard. See [Manage VPS cancellation requests](/faq/billing-lifecycle/manage-cancellation-requests) for the self-service steps.

  When you withdraw the cancellation, **EDIS Global re-issues renewal invoices** to ensure correct renewal and continuation of the service. Check **Billing → My Invoices** and your invoices folder. If in doubt, [ask support](https://www.edisglobal.com/contact).

  See the [VPS cancellation guide](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) and [Data retention periods](/faq/billing-lifecycle/data-retention-period).
</Note>

  ## Client responsibility notice

  As a VPS customer, you are solely responsible for:

  - Managing your virtual machines
  - Creating regular backups of your servers
  - Securing all important data
  - Keeping payment methods updated to avoid suspension and deletion

  EDIS Global strongly recommends maintaining backups of all critical information.
