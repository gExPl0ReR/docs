## Check your refund eligibility

You may request a refund for an eligible VPS order within **24 hours of purchase**. Refunds are subject to the conditions below and are not automatic.

<Info>
  Only one refund is available per customer within any 12-month period.
</Info>

## Consider a free location change

If you selected the wrong location, experience unsuitable latency, or need a different geographic presence, you may request a **one-time free location change** within 24 hours instead of cancelling.

A completed location change replaces the refund option. The VPS is no longer eligible for a refund after it has been reprovisioned in another location.

<CtaButton label="Learn About Location Changes" link="/faq/server-locations/change-server-location" />

## Refund eligibility checklist

Your request must satisfy all of these conditions:

- You purchased the VPS less than 24 hours ago.
- You have not already used your refund allowance within the past 12 months.
- You purchased the VPS without a special offer, discounted deal, or coupon code.
- You have not completed a free location change for the VPS.
- You have not revoked a cancellation request for the VPS.
- You have not added Pool Traffic to the VPS.
- You have not ordered a plan or resource upgrade for the VPS.
- The service was not closed for violating our terms or [Acceptable Use Policy](/getting-started/acceptable-use-policy).

## What to do next

If every condition above applies, cancel the VPS with immediate effect and submit the required IP address and Support PIN to our support team within the 24-hour window. Our team will review the request and confirm whether it qualifies.

<Warning>
  Cancelling at the end of the billing period does not satisfy the refund procedure. You must select immediate cancellation.
</Warning>
