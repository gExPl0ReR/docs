## How a VPS location change works

IP addresses and VPS resources are tied to a specific server location. EDIS Global therefore cannot physically move an existing VPS to another region.

For an approved location change, we provision a **direct replacement VPS** in another available EDIS Global location of your choice. The replacement becomes a new VPS service with new access and network details.

## Free location change within 24 hours

You may request **one free location change within 24 hours of the original VPS order**. This option is useful if you selected the wrong location, need lower latency, or require a different geographic presence.

### What you receive

When EDIS Global completes the location change:

- You receive a replacement VPS in another available location of your choice.
- You receive new login details and a new IP address.
- The replacement keeps the same VPS plan and billing cycle.
- The new VPS service directly replaces the original service.
- The original VPS, its IP addresses, and its data are permanently removed.

### Additional free primary IPv4 change

The location replacement also includes the option of **one additional free change of the new VPS's primary IPv4 address** if required.

After an IPv4 change, you must reinstall the VPS or configure the new address manually through VNC. See [Change IP Address](/faq/ip-addresses/change-ip-address) for the complete procedure.

### Refund eligibility after a location change

<Warning>
  A completed location change replaces the refund option. After EDIS Global provisions the replacement VPS, the original order and replacement service are no longer eligible for a refund.
</Warning>

A primary IPv4 change alone does not remove refund eligibility. A completed server location change does. See the [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy) for all conditions.

## Before requesting the location change

<Warning>
  Data, IP addresses, installed software, and configuration are not migrated to the replacement VPS. Back up everything you need before requesting the location change.
</Warning>

Location changes are:

- Available only within 24 hours of the original order.
- Limited to one location change per VPS service.
- Subject to resource availability in the requested location.
- Unavailable for services flagged for abuse, spam, fraud, or other policy violations.

Additional IPv4 addresses are not migrated automatically and may require a new order or approval. EDIS Global may decline a request for abuse prevention, fraud detection, resource availability, or policy enforcement reasons.

## Changing location after 24 hours

After the initial 24-hour period, the free location replacement is no longer available. To use a different location:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order a new VPS">
    Choose and order a VPS in the required location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Migrate your data">
    Transfer the data and configuration from the existing VPS to the new service.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cancel the old VPS">
    Cancel the previous VPS only after confirming that the new service works correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
