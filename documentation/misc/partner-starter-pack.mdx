## Network & traffic model

### Included bandwidth (per VPS)

Each VPS plan includes a **defined amount of monthly bandwidth**, depending on:

- Location
- Plan tier

You can **upgrade products at any time**.

---

### Traffic pool model (additional bandwidth)

In addition to per‑VPS included bandwidth, EDIS Global offers a **Traffic Pool** system:

- Shared traffic pool across all services in your account
- Traffic can be assigned manually or automatically
- Designed for high‑consumption or burst workloads
- Just one global price across all 50 EDIS locations.

#### Traffic assignment modes

**Manual Hydration**

- You assign traffic manually from the pool to a VPS

**Auto‑Hydration**

- The system automatically assigns traffic increments (e.g. 100 GB)
- Triggered when a VPS reaches ~99% of its included traffic
- Requires sufficient pool balance

Documentation: [https://docs.edisglobal.com/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool](https://docs.edisglobal.com/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool)

## IP addresses

- Additional IPv4: **€5 / IP**
- Random IP change: **€5 (NRC)**
- Bespoke IP change: **€10 (NRC)**

Every VPS includes its own **/64-equivalent IPv6 allocation**. This is not a separately routed `/64`: addresses use a `/48` prefix length to reach the location-specific gateway, and up to 20 stable IPv6 addresses may be configured simultaneously.

## Billing

### Billing cycles

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi‑annual
- Annual (1 month free)

Hourly billing is **not** available.

### Payment methods

- Credit Card
- PayPal
- Cryptocurrency
- Apple Pay, Google Pay and Alipay
- SEPA / ACH Direct Debit (by invitation only)
- SEPA / SWIFT bank transfer (manually processed; account-credit top-ups of at least EUR 250)

<Warning>
  Bank transfers are not suitable for recurring monthly payments, urgent renewals or immediate provisioning. Direct payment of an invoice by bank transfer requires prior agreement and is reserved for emergencies when no automated payment method is available.

  Sending a transfer does not mark an invoice as paid. Until our accounting team has received, identified and manually assigned it, the normal non-payment workflow continues and can lead to service suspension, termination and permanent data deletion.
</Warning>
